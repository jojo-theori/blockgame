# Holo foils

The files in `css/` and `img/` are from
[simeydotme/pokemon-cards-css](https://github.com/simeydotme/pokemon-cards-css)
by Simon Goellner, used under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (see
`LICENSE` in this folder). Image paths in the stylesheets were changed to
point at `../img/`; nothing else in them was changed.

`index.css` and `foils.js` in this folder are ours: the base of the two
layers those stylesheets expect, and which foil each rank gets.

Because of these files this project as a whole is offered under the
GPL-3.0 (see `LICENSE` at the top of the repository).
